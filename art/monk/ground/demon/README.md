# Chapter 5 ground: Demon Peak (2026-10-04)

- **Tile**: `cave_src.png`, generated from `cave.txt` (its embers asked for cold blue, not
  violet), cropped to its inner square 180,60-880,760 past the vignette and made seamless by
  `tools/make_tile.py --value 0.40 --sat 0.3 --contrast 0.21` (see the retune below) into
  `client/public/art/ch5/ground/cave.png`. Before it was generated the floor was drawn in code
  by `tools/rock_tile.py --rgb 50,52,58 --ember 120,92,180 --embers 10` (Voronoi rock plates on a
  torus); kept as a fallback.
- **Props**: brazier, spikes, bones, stupa and crystals, generated by text-to-image from the
  `*.txt` prompts here. They came back with violet glows (flames, crystals, skull eyes) and
  violet is kept for enemy attacks, so `cool_glow.py` turns saturated violet cold blue into
  `*_v2.png`. Packed (as `<name>.png` copies of the `_v2`, the `_v3` for crystals and brazier) by `tools/pack_deco.py` into
  `client/public/art/ch5/ground/cave_deco.{png,json}` (`PEAK_DECO` in
  `client/src/game/decoView.ts`).
- **Retuned 2026-10-20** because the horde got lost on the floor. The first grade
  (`--value 0.24 --sat 0.7 --contrast 0.6`) gave a dark, busy cave: ground L* 9/18/29
  (p10/50/90) with deep cracks on 4.5% of it, right where the mobs' black hair and outlines sit
  (a fifth of their pixels are below L* 20), and its cracks drew false edges through them. The
  bright cyan crystals and the cauldron's flame also outshone the teal robes. Now the floor is
  flattened to about a third of its spread, lifted to a mid stone (L* 23/31/42, between the ink
  and the robes) and nearly grey, so the mobs read as dark silhouettes with teal fills; and
  `dim_props.py` (saturation x0.4, brightness spread x0.45 around the stone's, ink kept) turns
  `crystals_v2`/`brazier_v2` into `crystals_v3`/`brazier_v3`, packed in their place. Mob pixels
  within dE 15 of the ground behind them, on a paused phone frame: 37.5% to 26.4% in the
  160-mob sandbox and 32.1% to 19.1% on wave 12; false edges 4.5% to 1.4%.
