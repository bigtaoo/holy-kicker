# Chapter 2 ground: the misty marsh (2026-10-03)

Generated by text-to-image from the `*.txt` prompts here, like the temple's grass and props
(`../deco/README.md`).

- **Tile**: `marsh2.png` (the first try, `marsh.png`, was a framed scene with one big pool,
  and `marsh3.png` had a leafy border too). The inner square 200,90-800,690 went through
  `tools/make_tile.py --value 0.27 --sat 0.45 --hue-shift 20 --contrast 0.45` into
  `client/public/art/ground/marsh.png` (copy: `tile_marsh.png`). Lower contrast than the
  grass: the puddles and pale leaves are busier, and at the grass settings they pulled the
  eye away from the mobs.
- **Props**: reeds, puddle, lily, mossrock, log, lantern, packed by
  `tools/pack_deco.py` and renamed to `client/public/art/ground/marsh_deco.{png,json}`.
  The puddle and the lily pads lie flat and are drawn as decals (`MARSH_DECO` in
  `client/src/game/decoView.ts`); reeds are the most common prop. The first lantern came out
  as a little shrine house; asking for a lantern "on a pillar, about twice as tall as wide" fixed it.
- In a wave 3 run the grey-blue water ghosts and purple toads stay clear against the mud and
  puddles, and no prop reads as an enemy or a pickup.
