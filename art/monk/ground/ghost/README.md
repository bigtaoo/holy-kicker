# Chapter 4 ground: the Ghost Market (2026-10-04)

- **Tile**: no generated picture. `tools/flagstone_tile.py --rgb 62,68,86` draws the night
  street in code: staggered rows of uneven slate flagstones with dark seams, one hard cel
  lip each, a few cracks, puddles and paper scraps, seamless by construction, into
  `client/public/art/ch4/ground/street.png`. `street.txt` is the prompt for a generated one.
- **Props**: stall, jars and burner, generated by text-to-image from the `*.txt` prompts here
  (the `*_v0.png` were warm or off-style rejects) and packed by `tools/pack_deco.py` into
  `client/public/art/ch4/ground/street_deco.{png,json}` (`GHOST_DECO` in
  `client/src/game/decoView.ts`). The lantern post, paper offerings and crates were
  rate-limited; `GHOST_DECO` already lists them and kinds missing from the sheet are left
  out, so repacking with them is all that is needed.
