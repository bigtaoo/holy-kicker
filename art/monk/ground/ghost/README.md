# Chapter 4 ground: the Ghost Market (2026-10-04)

- **Tile**: no generated picture. `tools/flagstone_tile.py --rgb 90,86,82 --seam 0.75` draws
  the night street in code: staggered rows of uneven flagstones with dark seams, one hard cel
  lip each, a few cracks, puddles and paper scraps, seamless by construction, into
  `client/public/art/ch4/ground/street.png`. `street.txt` is the prompt for a generated one.
- **Retuned 2026-10-18** because players found the chapter too dark to see. The first tile
  (`--rgb 62,68,86`, seams at 0.55) was a dark slate blue, the same hue as the teal-robed,
  purple-haired ghosts, and its near-black seam grid matched their hair and outlines.
  Counting mob pixels within dE 15 of the ground behind them, it hid 34% of the horde on a
  paused wave-15 phone frame (23% on desktop), against 12-21% in chapters 1 and 2, whose
  cold mobs stand on olive ground. A warm grey stone, a bit lighter (mean L* 27 to 35) with
  shallower seams, plus a warm grey tint on the blue props (`GHOST_DECO`), brought the same
  frame to 27% (19%) and lets the ghosts read by hue; the cyan lantern-light patches keep it night.
- **Props**: stall, jars and burner, generated by text-to-image from the `*.txt` prompts here
  (the `*_v0.png` were warm or off-style rejects) and packed by `tools/pack_deco.py` into
  `client/public/art/ch4/ground/street_deco.{png,json}` (`GHOST_DECO` in
  `client/src/game/decoView.ts`). The lantern post, paper offerings and crates were
  rate-limited at first and added later; the paper offerings took three tries (`papers_v0`
  pale with no outline, `papers_v1` filling the whole frame).
