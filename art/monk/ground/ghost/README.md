# Chapter 4 ground: the Ghost Market (2026-10-04)

- **Tile**: no generated picture. `tools/flagstone_tile.py --rgb 64,58,52 --seam 0.75 --relief 0.45` draws
  the night street in code: staggered rows of uneven flagstones with dark seams, one hard cel
  lip each, a few cracks, puddles and paper scraps, seamless by construction, into
  `client/public/art/ch4/ground/street.png`. `street.txt` is the prompt for a generated one.
- **Retuned 2026-10-18** because players found the chapter too dark to see. The first tile
  (`--rgb 62,68,86`, seams at 0.55) was a dark slate blue, the same hue as the teal-robed,
  purple-haired ghosts, and its near-black seam grid matched their hair and outlines.
  Counting mob pixels within dE 15 of the ground behind them, it hid 34% of the horde on a
  paused wave-15 phone frame (23% on desktop), against 12-21% in chapters 1 and 2, whose
  cold mobs stand on olive ground. A warm grey stone, a bit lighter (mean L* 27 to 35) with
  shallower seams, plus a warm grey tint on the blue props (`GHOST_DECO`), brought the same
  frame to 27% (19%) and lets the ghosts read by hue; the cyan lantern-light patches keep it night.
  (That tile was `--rgb 90,86,82 --seam 0.75`.)
- **Retuned 2026-10-20**: with the hue settled, the street still sat at the ghosts' own
  lightness (ground L* median 30 to their 41-44) and its lips, puddles and scraps were busy
  enough to break their outlines. A darker warm grey (`--rgb 64,58,52`, ground L* 17/21/36) with
  every tone pulled to under half its distance from the slab (`--relief 0.45`, tile luma spread
  10 to 4) puts the teal robes clearly above the floor: body contrast |dL*| 19.6 to 22.0, mob
  pixels within dE 15 of the ground on a paused phone frame 21.4% to 17.7% (160-mob sandbox)
  and 17.1% to 16.4% on wave 12, where the light patches and props already carried most of it.
- **Props**: stall, jars and burner, generated by text-to-image from the `*.txt` prompts here
  (the `*_v0.png` were warm or off-style rejects) and packed by `tools/pack_deco.py` into
  `client/public/art/ch4/ground/street_deco.{png,json}` (`GHOST_DECO` in
  `client/src/game/decoView.ts`). The lantern post, paper offerings and crates were
  rate-limited at first and added later; the paper offerings took three tries (`papers_v0`
  pale with no outline, `papers_v1` filling the whole frame).

## Crisper, warmer street (2026-11-19)

Redrawn at 1024 px (the game draws a 1024 tile over the same span as a 512 one,
`stageArt.ts` `groundScale`), warmer sand grey so the teal and violet ghosts stand off it, with
hairline cracks, chipped corners and pebbles (`--wear`, its own random stream, so the slabs
keep their layout):

```
python tools/flagstone_tile.py raw.png --size 1024 --rgb 64,58,52 --seam 0.7 --relief 0.7 --wear 3 --colors 96
python tools/make_tile.py raw.png client/public/art/ch4/ground/street.png whole --size 1024 --value 0.52 --sat 1.4 --contrast 0.9 --hue-shift 4
```

The monk's street also scatters more props (`GHOST_DECO.fill` 0.85, and small loose spirit
papers besides the big piles); the corgi's keeps the shared density.
