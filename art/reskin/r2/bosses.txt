Game character design sheet in a bright cheerful cartoon sticker style: thick black outlines, flat colours, one hard cel shadow, chunky chibi proportions, readable at small size. Plain white background, each character standing apart, full body, three-quarter front view, no overlap. No text, no letters, no labels.
All enemies are alien bugs in cold colours only (teal, purple, grey-blue, dark slate) with glowing red eyes, cute-menacing rather than gross, no gore.
Five boss monsters for a horde shooter, each huge and distinct, in a row:
1. A titan beetle with a massive armoured back, raising its front legs to slam the ground.
2. A giant sand worm bursting up from the ground with a round mouth full of teeth.
3. A hive queen with a long egg sac, a crown-like crest and wings, summoning small grubs.
4. A floating giant alien brain overmind in a cracked glass dome with psychic tentacles.
5. An evil cyborg corgi clone in black and purple armour with a red robotic eye and a dark blaster, the villain twin of the hero (this one uses dark purple, black and grey only).
