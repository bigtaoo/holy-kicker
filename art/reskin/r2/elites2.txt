Game boss design sheet in a bright cheerful cartoon sticker style: thick black outlines, flat colours, one hard cel shadow, chunky stylised proportions, readable at small size. Plain white background, each creature standing apart, full body, three-quarter top-down view as in a top-down shooter. No text, no letters, no labels.
Strictly cold colours only: teal, purple, grey-blue, dark slate, with glowing red eyes. No orange, no pink, no red bodies, no yellow, no skin tones. Real alien insect anatomy (six legs, segmented carapace, mandibles), not humanoid, no armour suits, no hands. Cute-menacing, no gore.
Four elite bugs, each big and heavy:
1. Ram Beetle: a teal rhinoceros beetle with one huge battering-ram horn, lowered for a charge.
2. Brood Mother: a bloated purple bug with a huge swollen glowing teal abdomen dripping acid and three spitting mouths.
3. Alpha Mantis: a big grey-blue mantis with a frilled crest, scythe arms raised, screeching.
4. Fortress Crab-Bug: a slate-blue armoured crab-beetle hiding behind one giant flat shield claw, the other claw a hammer.
