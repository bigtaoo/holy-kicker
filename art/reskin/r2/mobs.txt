Game character design sheet in a bright cheerful cartoon sticker style: thick black outlines, flat colours, one hard cel shadow, chunky chibi proportions, readable at small size. Plain white background, each character standing apart, full body, three-quarter front view, no overlap. No text, no letters, no labels.
All enemies are alien bugs in cold colours only (teal, purple, grey-blue, dark slate) with glowing red eyes, cute-menacing rather than gross, no gore.
Six small regular enemy types for a horde shooter, in a row, similar size:
1. A round armoured beetle drone walking on six legs, the basic grunt.
2. A tiny fast spider-tick with long legs, leaning forward as if sprinting.
3. A segmented burrowing worm popping half out of a hole in the ground, jaws open.
4. A fat spitter bug with a bulging acid sac on its back and a nozzle-like mouth.
5. A lean mantis hunter with scythe arms, crouched to pounce.
6. A floating jellyfish-like psychic bug with a glowing purple brain dome and dangling tendrils.
