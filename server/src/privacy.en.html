Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-10-22  ·  Holy Kicker  ·  support@gamestao.com  ·  中文

This policy explains what data the game Holy Kicker collects, why, and what you can do about it. It applies wherever you play the game, on our own site, on game portals such as CrazyGames and in the iPhone app. We follow the EU General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) and the applicable data protection law.

There is no account and no sign-up. Your progress and settings stay on your device (on CrazyGames, in the portal's own save). The game sends two things to our own server: gameplay statistics and leaderboard runs, linked to a random play ID rather than to who you are. If you choose to back up your progress (Settings → Cloud save), a copy of your save is kept on our server too. In the EEA, the UK and Switzerland, nothing is sent until you allow it. Everywhere else sending is on, and you can switch it off at any time: Settings → Play data → Sharing.

1. Who is responsible

The controller is Tao Wang, an independent sole trader (not a company) based in Germany, reachable at support@gamestao.com.

3. What we do not collect

  • No email address, no password, no account.
  • No third-party analytics or tracking. The statistics above go to our own server only. The game itself contains no Google Analytics, no Firebase and no tracking script. The iPhone app contains Google's ad software for the ads described in §5, limited to non-personalised ads.
  • No advertising ID and no profile of you, and no tracking across other sites or apps. The iPhone app never asks to track you, so neither we nor Google get Apple's advertising ID.
  • No location finer than your country (§2, from your IP address with the gameplay statistics, so only once you allow them). On CrazyGames the portal also tells the game your country, which is used on your device only to decide whether to ask before sending anything. Google may estimate a rough location from your IP address to serve ads in the iPhone app (§5).
  • No payment details. The only thing sold for real money is the iPhone app's Ad-free card, bought through Apple's App Store. Apple handles the payment; we never see your card, your Apple ID or your name.
  • No sale or sharing of your data for anyone else's purposes.

4. Stored on your device

The game keeps a few items in your browser's local storage (on CrazyGames, in the portal's save, which follows a signed-in player to other devices; in the iPhone app, also copied into the app's own storage, so iOS clearing the web storage loses nothing). No cookies.

  • hk.save: your progress
  • hk.settings: your settings, including your answer about play data
  • hk.run: a run left unfinished, so you can continue it
  • hk.install: the random play ID. It is only created once something is sent, so if you never allow sharing, it never exists. It is shown in Settings → Play data.
  • hk.named: the leaderboard name last sent, so it is not sent again
  • hk.cloud: your restore code, once you back up or restore (§2)
  • hk.adFree (iPhone app only): whether you own the Ad-free card, as the App Store last told the app

5. Advertising

On our own site the game shows no ads. On CrazyGames, ads are served by CrazyGames: optional rewarded videos, a short break ad between runs, and a banner in the lobby. CrazyGames' privacy policy covers those ads and any consent they need. We receive no advertising ID and nothing about you from them, only whether an ad finished.

In the iPhone app, ads are served by Google AdMob: optional rewarded videos and a short break ad between runs. Every request asks for non-personalised ads only, and the app never shows Apple's tracking prompt. In the EEA, the UK and Switzerland, Google's consent form asks you first, and Settings → Play data → Ad privacy opens it again. To serve, count and protect these ads Google processes your IP address, an identifier that only this developer's apps share, which ads you saw and whether you tapped them, and technical and crash data from its ad software, as described in how Google uses information from apps that use its services. With the Ad-free card no ad is shown; its rewards are paid at once.

6. Who else processes the data

  • The game portal you play on (for example CrazyGames). It hosts the page, saves your progress, tells the game your username if you are signed in, and serves ads: CrazyGames privacy policy.
  • Apple, in the iPhone app: the App Store, the purchase of the Ad-free card, and Game Center, which gives the game your nickname if you are signed in: Apple privacy policy.
  • Google, in the iPhone app: the ads (§5): Google privacy policy.
  • Cloudflare: delivers the game and carries the traffic to our server.
  • Hetzner Online (Germany): runs our server.
  • MongoDB Atlas: hosts the database.

They process the data for us under data processing agreements. Where one of them processes data outside the EEA, it does so under the European Commission's standard contractual clauses.

7. How long we keep it

  • Gameplay events: 90 days, then the database deletes them.
  • Problem reports: 180 days, then deleted.
  • Run replays: 15 days, then deleted.
  • Cloud saves: 30 days after the copy was last updated, then deleted. Every launch of a device that backs up updates it, so a backup you keep using stays; one left alone for 30 days is gone.
  • Leaderboard entries, board names and one summary per play ID (the first day played, the days played since, the platform, the first game version, the device, browser and system families and the country, and how many days and runs each first chapter clear took): as long as the leaderboards run, or until you ask us to delete them.

8. Your rights

You can ask for access to your data, correction, deletion, restriction, a portable copy, and object to processing. Where we rely on your consent you can withdraw it at any time, as easily as you gave it: Settings → Play data → Sharing: Off stops all sending at once, and events not yet sent are discarded.

Because we do not know who you are, we can only find your data by your play ID. To see or delete it, email support@gamestao.com with the play ID shown in Settings (for a cloud save, with its restore code). We answer within 30 days, free of charge. You also have the right to complain to a data protection authority.

9. Children

The game asks no one for personal data: there is no account, no chat and nothing to type except an optional problem report. If you believe a child has sent us personal data in a report, contact us and we will delete it.
